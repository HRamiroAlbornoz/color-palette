## Overall Impression

The restraint is real and holds up under actual use (six size/theme/interaction combinations tested live, zero console errors throughout). The single biggest opportunity is closing the gap between what the direction contract promised and what shipped: a header with no data, a grid one column short of spec, and - more seriously - a genuine WCAG AA failure hiding inside the app's own stated differentiator (the technical data shown per swatch).

## What's Working

1. The lock mechanic is real, not decorative. Verified end-to-end: toggling lock, regenerating, and the locked swatch neither moves nor changes while siblings refresh around it - this is the one place the brief's thesis is fully realized.
2. Defensive data handling matches the ADR. storage.js validates every field's shape and range by hand before trusting anything from localStorage; corrupted or disabled storage degrades to a clean empty state instead of crashing.
3. Restraint holds across conditions. The generated palette stays the only saturated thing on screen consistently across light/dark and all three sizes tested - a harder discipline to maintain than it looks.

## Priority Issues

[P0] WCAG AA contrast failure on the product's core differentiator data
- Why it matters: css/components.css:171-173 dims the "HEX"/"HSL"/"AA" labels inside every swatch via opacity: 0.7, blending the already-computed high-contrast label color toward the swatch's own random background. Independently verified across 52,128 sampled colors spanning the app's actual generation range: the dimmed label drops below 4.5:1 AA in 44.6% of cases, down to 2.86:1 in the worst case - while the full-opacity value text next to it stays compliant. The exact thing this product is positioned to differentiate on ("technical data travels alongside the color... evaluated without leaving the tool") has its own field labels unreadable on nearly half of what it generates.
- Fix: drop the opacity trick for text sitting on a dynamic background. Differentiate label from value with weight or size, not alpha - the surrounding text-color logic already guarantees >=4.58:1 at full opacity, so simply removing the opacity: 0.7 declaration restores AA everywhere.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

## Persona Red Flags

Alex (Power User): the entire value proposition is "generate -> look -> lock -> regenerate -> copy" in under a minute, yet there's no keyboard acceleration beyond default tab order (heuristic #7 above). Locking swatch 5 of 9 means tabbing past the size radios, format radios, and every preceding swatch/lock-button pair - real, avoidable friction against the tool's own "speed over explanation" mandate.

Sam (Accessibility-Dependent User): hit hardest by the P0 contrast bug - the contrast-ratio label is the thing this persona most needs to trust, and it's the thing most likely to be unreadable. Compounding it: js/render.js:135-145 gives every batch's Restore/Delete buttons identical accessible names ("Restaurar"/"Borrar") with no aria-label disambiguation - a screen-reader user with several saved batches hears repeated, indistinguishable "Restaurar, button / Borrar, button" pairs with no way to tell which batch a control belongs to without backtracking.
